Commentary
I chose this video analysis because I love how it breaks down the core musical structure behind Latin rhythms and salsa arrangements. Rather than just playing a song, the creator systematically analyzes how the conga, cowbell, piano tumbao, and clave pattern interact to build the rhythm. It provides a deep technical look at how musical elements work together, which is a perfect fit for an analytical paper.
